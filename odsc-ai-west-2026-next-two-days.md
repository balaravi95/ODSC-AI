# ODSC AI West 2026 — Two-Day Schedule

**Prepared:** October 27, 2026  
**Plan:** Wednesday, October 28 and Thursday, October 29, 2026  
**Venue:** Hyatt Regency Burlingame, California  
**Time zone:** Pacific Time (PT)

This is a recommended, engineering-focused path through the live agenda. ODSC has
multiple tracks running in parallel, so the selections below prioritize agentic AI,
coding agents, evaluation/observability, and production systems while leaving room
for networking and the expo.

### Wednesday alternatives

- Replace the 10:00 AM workshop with **Reimagine your Mainframe Systems with
  Agentic AI** (Track 2, 10:00–10:30 AM) if modernization is more relevant.
- At 11:00 AM, choose **Local-First AI: Building Responsive, Private, and
  Cost-Efficient AI Applications at the Edge** (Regency B) or the two-hour
  **From Retrieval to Recall: Building Governed Agentic Memory on Vector
  Databases** training (Regency C).
- At 3:30 PM, the more hands-on alternatives are **Build Your Own Self-Improving
  Agent with OpenClaw and MLflow** (Regency C, 3:30–4:30 PM) and **The Design of
  Coding Agents: A Hands-On Workshop on Harness Engineering** (Regency A,
  3:30–5:30 PM). These overlap with the Opik training, so choose one route.

### Thursday alternatives

- Choose **Agentic Time Series Forecasting** (Track 1, 12:55–1:40 PM) instead
  of the production-agents training if forecasting is a priority.
- Choose **LeRobot: Democratizing Real World Physical AI** (Track 1,
  1:45–2:30 PM) for a physical-AI focus.
- Choose **Causal ML for AI Evaluation: Calibrating LLM Judges to Real World
  Value** (Regency B, 2:00–3:00 PM) for an evaluation/measurement focus.
- The **Startup Pitch & Showcase** runs 11:30 AM–1:00 PM and is a good swap for
  the fine-tuning session if founder and product discovery is the priority.

## Practical checklist

1. Confirm room availability and any pass restrictions in the live schedule before
   leaving for the venue; workshops and training sessions can have limited capacity.
2. Keep at least 10 minutes for room changes. The schedule has simultaneous sessions
   across Track 1, Track 2, Regency rooms, Grand Peninsula rooms, and the Expo Area.
3. Use the expo blocks for vendor conversations and demos rather than sacrificing
   the longer training sessions.
4. Capture one actionable idea, one contact, and one follow-up question per major
   session; reserve the final networking block for those follow-ups.
